Trust is not a promise at SKYGLOBEGROUP. It's the architecture.

🛡️

No verification, no trade

Every participant passes TERRA identity verification — a real document and a live selfie check — before they can trade. Not optional. Ever.

🔒

Escrow-protected deals

Funds are held safely and released to the seller only when the buyer confirms delivery — with a fair Resolution Centre if anything goes wrong.

⭐

Reviews that can't be faked

A review can only be written by the buyer of a completed deal — once. Every star on this platform was earned through real trade.

🔐

Secured end-to-end

Encrypted in transit and at rest, hashed passwords, private identity storage and a full audit trail. Your data is never publicly exposed.

🌍

Credentials the world can check

Certificates and trust marks carry lifetime QR verification — any employer, partner or bank can confirm them from anywhere.

🤝

Honest by doctrine

No invented numbers, no fake activity, no pretending. Settlement runs through licensed partners under each country's law.
