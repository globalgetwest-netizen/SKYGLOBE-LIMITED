ℹ️ About this portal

This is the CEO Portal — private to you. It includes full business analytics, application data, CSV exports, client messaging and document management. Staff members log in separately at /staff using a different password, where they can handle applications and messages but cannot see analytics or export data. All data is securely stored and only accessible with the correct password.
