The ECO Currency

Africa's single-currency ambition

💶 ECO · West African single currency

What is the ECO?

The ECO is the proposed single currency for West Africa, championed by the Economic Community of West African States (ECOWAS) — a bloc of 15 nations. Its goal is to deepen trade and integration across the region, ease cross-border business and payments, and give West Africa greater monetary independence.

Backed by
ECOWAS · 15 member states
Status
Proposed — not yet in circulation
Would replace
West African CFA franc (XOF) & others
Target
Adoption planned for 2027

The plan has faced repeated delays and debate. In 2019, the francophone (UEMOA) countries announced renaming the CFA franc the "Eco" and reforms — including ending the requirement to hold reserves with the French treasury — which caused tension with the non-CFA West African Monetary Zone (WAMZ) countries (led by Nigeria and Ghana) who favour a broader, jointly-governed currency. Convergence criteria on inflation, deficits and debt must be met before launch.
