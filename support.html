Frequently asked questions

What is SKYGLOBEGROUP?
One connected ecosystem — NORIA (intelligence), TERRA (trust & verification), YUNEX (commerce), Academy (learning) and Mobility (travel). One SKYGLOBE ID works across all of them.
How do I verify my account?
Create a SKYGLOBE ID, then confirm your email using the link or code we send you. For full trust, complete TERRA identity verification from your SKYGLOBE ID page.
How does payment and escrow work on YUNEX?
Agree a price with a verified seller, pay into secure escrow, and funds are released to the seller only when you confirm delivery.
Are the certificates from Academy recognised?
Each completed programme issues a TERRA-verified digital certificate with a unique verification reference that anyone can check.
How do I become a verified seller?
Complete TERRA identity (and business) verification from your SKYGLOBE ID. No verification, no trade — this protects every buyer.
